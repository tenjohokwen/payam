Third wave of Phase 65: move the payment-domain types out of the catch-all `common` package and into their owning bounded context `payment.core.contract`. This is the single highest-blast-radius wave — `MobilePaymentProvider` alone is imported by ~28 production files plus ~28 test files, making this the dominant import frequency in the codebase.

- `common.payment` (6 files): `MobileMoneyPort`, `MobilePaymentProvider`, `PaymentCommand`, `PaymentMethod`, `ProviderResult`, `SubscriberStatus` — these define the hexagonal port contract between `payment.core` (the application core) and `payment.provider.{mtn,orange}` (the adapters).

Both destinations are `payment.core.contract` per RESEARCH.md decision (Open Question 1): keeping refund alongside payment in `contract` is consistent with CMN-01's destination mapping and zero callers means no risk to that decision.

- The cross-cutting infrastructure imports inside the 9 files-to-move (if any) are already retargeted.
