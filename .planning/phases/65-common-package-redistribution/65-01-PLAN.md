<objective>
First infrastructure move wave of Phase 65. Relocate the four "foundational" common sub-packages — `common.exception` (7 files), `common.message` (6 files), `common.config` (2 files), `common.logging` (2 files) — to their respective `infrastructure.*` sub-packages. Update all external callers' imports plus the single FQN body reference in `PlatformConfigServiceTest.java` line 258.

These four subpackages are chosen for Wave 1 because they have NO dependencies on other common subpackages (verified by grep — `common.logging.InventoryCode` depends only on `common.exception.ErrorCode`, which is moving with it in the same atomic commit). They are also the highest-volume "type-only" dependencies — ErrorCode alone is imported by 25 production files — making this the foundational wave that unblocks Plan 02 (utility layer) from depending on common at all.

Purpose: Phase 65 is the final v12 architectural reorganization step. The `common` package — historically a catch-all — must be fully emptied and removed. Wave 1 plants the new `infrastructure.exception`, `infrastructure.message`, and `infrastructure.logging` sub-packages and expands `infrastructure.config` (already created in Phase 61 with AsyncConfig/DataSourceConfig/ObservabilityConfig) by joining CommonConfig + LongFromStringDeserializer to it.

Output: 17 new production files under `infrastructure.*`; old `common/exception/`, `common/message/`, `common/config/`, `common/logging/` directories deleted; ~29 caller files updated (28 prod + 1 test); `PlatformConfigServiceTest.java` FQN body reference fixed; `mvn verify` green (BUILD-01). After this plan, `common/` still contains payment, refund, client, threadpool, util, validation, consumer, dto, enums, and 6 root-level files — those land in Plans 02-04.
